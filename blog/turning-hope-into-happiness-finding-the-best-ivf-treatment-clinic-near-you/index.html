Some Tips To Increase Your Chances Of Pregnancy

Maintain A Balanced Diet

In infertility and IVF, maintaining a balanced diet is important because obesity and being underweight can decrease the chances of conceiving and lower the IVF success rate. The other side effect of being overweight is that it becomes difficult to evaluate ovaries during the IVF process. Extra weight can increase the risk of difficulties during the egg retrieval procedure; with the consultation of an expert, changes in nutrition and activity are the best way to address this.

Quit Smoking

Smoking decreases the quality of eggs and sperm, as it can reduce the chances of conception when you are undergoing IVF treatment. Quitting smoking can be a difficult task, but with the right support, you will be able to quit smoking. If you combine your personal determination with professional help, then there are stronger chances of staying smoke-free.

Relax And Unwind

Stress can have a negative impact on both, whether you are trying to conceive naturally or with the help of IVF treatment. Overcoming stress throughout the IVF procedure can be the most difficult task for couples to attain. You can follow stress-relieving methods, such as enrolling in a mind-body program or attending stress resilience training seminars, which can help improve pregnancy outcomes.

Optimize Sperm Health

Having healthy sperm is a crucial part of fertility, and multivitamins, as well as eating antioxidant-rich foods, can help to boost sperm quality. Optimizing sperm health can have a great impact on IVF treatment, provided it is treated with great care.

Stay Positive and Patient

Some couples may require more IVF cycles to get an embryo for transfer, and the outcome may vary from cycle to cycle. Unfortunately, if your first IVF cycle was not successful, then your health care provider may change your medications to make the next one better.
