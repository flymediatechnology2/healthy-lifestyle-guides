How Are Warts Removed By Dermatologists?

Different types of warts

Before removing warts, it's important to understand the exact type of wart. Warts fall into different categories on the basis of location and size. Basically, warts appear on hands and fingers and feel rough to the touch.

Identifying the type helps the doctor to choose the best approach for treatment. To remove the warts based on type, the following treatment options are available.

Cryotherapy: freezing the wart

It's a common method used by skin specialists to remove warts. During the procedure, liquid nitrogen is directly applied to the wart. The cold destroys the wart tissue. This treatment takes only a few seconds per wart. A cotton swab or spray is used by dermatologists. You may feel some sensation that improves quickly.

When treatment is finished, there is a chance of forming a blister around the wart. This is part of the treatment step. It lifts the dead tissue away from the skin and falls off within one or two weeks.

Laser treatment

It is a high-tech option to remove warts in difficult cases. The laser treatment is used to destroy the blood supply and blood vessels feeding the wart. Before the laser treatment, the skin is numbed, and the treatment is done without affecting healthy tissue; only the affected tissue is destroyed with the laser to prevent the growth of warts.

The area gets numb before treatment. This approach is preferred for warts under nails, sensitive skin or the feet. The wart becomes dark in color because the tissue dies and the blood supply dies; sometimes multiple laser sessions are required to prevent the growth of warts. This treatment is suggested if drugstore remedies or other remedies are not effective.

Surgical excision

For large and deep warts, a surgical approach is recommended by doctors to destroy the growth of warts. Before making the cut with a margin of healthy tissue, the skin is first numbed so that you will not feel the pain or sensations. This provides immediate removal and may leave a small scar.

This method is only used by the best skin doctor in Ludhiana when other options are not effective.

Conclusion

Understanding how dermatologists remove warts helps you to take care of your skin health. Lasers and freezing are modern dermatology methods that offer various effective options. Dermatologists suggest the right treatment method based on your overall condition and the growth of warts because some warts can be treated with laser treatments or surgical methods.
